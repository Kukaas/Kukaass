# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and potential freelance clients arriving from a
résumé link, a search for "Chester Luke Maligaso" / "Kukaass", or a referral.
They land with low patience and a single question: *is this person credible and
worth contacting?* Recruiters scan fast for signal (stack, seniority, proof of
shipped work). Clients weigh trust and whether they'd hand a project over. Both
decide in seconds, often on mobile, and most are not reading the code.

The job to be done: let a visitor confirm Chester is a serious full-stack
developer, see real work that backs it, and reach out, without friction.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that establishes Chester Luke A. Maligaso ("Kukaass") as a
credible full-stack developer and builds personal brand. It showcases projects,
experience, and stack, offers a downloadable résumé, an AI chat assistant that
answers questions about his background, and a contact path. An admin CMS lets
Chester manage projects, experience, and résumés without redeploying.

Success: a visitor leaves convinced of competence and reaches out (message,
résumé download, or remembers the "Kukaass" name).

## Brand Personality

Confident, clean, and quietly senior. Three words: **precise, polished,
unpretentious.** The voice is plainspoken and specific, it names what was built
and which problems were solved, never reaching for hype. The interface should
feel like a well-made product (Linear / Vercel polish): crisp dark surface,
restrained depth, one cool accent, tight grid, deliberate motion. The work
carries the impression; the chrome stays out of the way.

## Anti-references

- **The generic AI / template portfolio.** Gradient text, floating blurred
  "orbs", glassmorphism as the default card, blue→cyan gradient accents on an
  untouched default-shadcn grayscale palette, and buzzword copy ("cutting-edge",
  "transforming ideas", "elegant, scalable"). The current site leans on these;
  the redesign should move away from them.
- **Brutalism.** Minimal does not mean raw, harsh, or anti-design. No exposed
  borders-as-aesthetic, monospace-everything, or deliberately crude layout.
- **Loud / over-animated.** No neon, particles, or constant ambient movement
  competing with the content.
- **Corporate / agency stiff.** No stock photography, sales-y hero, or soulless
  "enterprise" tone.

## Design Principles

- **The work is the argument.** Projects and shipped results carry credibility;
  the design frames them and gets out of the way. Show proof, don't claim it.
- **Quiet authority over flash.** Refinement, spacing, and typographic
  hierarchy signal seniority more convincingly than effects ever could.
- **Decide in seconds.** Optimize the first screen and mobile for a fast
  credibility read; a recruiter should "get it" before scrolling.
- **One honest accent.** A single, deliberate accent color does the work of
  emphasis. No rainbow gradients, no decoration for its own sake.
- **Specific, not promotional.** Copy names real technologies, real outcomes,
  and real roles. Remove marketing adjectives.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1 and large/UI text ≥3:1 against their
backgrounds (the current muted-gray-on-dark needs verification, several grays
are borderline). Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support, every ambient or
entrance animation needs a reduced/static alternative. Keyboard-navigable with
visible focus states, semantic landmarks, and meaningful link/button labels.
The site is dark-only today; keep contrast honest within that constraint.
